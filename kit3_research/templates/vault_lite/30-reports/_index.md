# Reports

Finished deliverables. Each one is dated, has a reader, and states its confidence.

Reports are **records**: they say what was true when they were written. Do not silently update an
old report to match new facts — write a new one and link back. A report that quietly changed is a
report nobody can cite.

## Naming

`report_<topic>_<yyyy-mm-dd>.md`
