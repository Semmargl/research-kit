# Capture

Raw sources, one note per source. Written by the `capture_url` skill.

Nothing here is a conclusion. A capture note records what a source says, including claims you
doubt — the doubt goes into `## Open questions`, not into a deletion.

## Subfolders

- `articles/` — blogs, documentation, news
- `papers/` — academic papers and preprints
- `videos/` — talks, podcasts, recorded sessions

## Extraction

Each capture carries an `extraction_deadline` (default: seven days). By then, either its reusable
insight has moved into `10-reference/` or a report, or the note is archived as read-and-not-useful.

A capture folder that only grows is a reading list wearing a knowledge base costume.
